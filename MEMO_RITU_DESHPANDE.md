---

### 1. The Decision

Do not shut down or penalize all new service partners. Most of our 60 new regional partners are honest and essential for our Tier-2 and Tier-3 expansion. 

Instead, we have made two targeted decisions:
1. **Fix the May 1 Loophole Immediately:** When we started auto-approving claims under ₹2,000 without inspection, honest repairs sped up, but seven rogue service partners immediately began submitting dozens of fake ₹1,800–₹1,950 claims. We must close this loophole for unverified partners.
2. **Deploy Priority Queue for Farhan’s Desk:** Farhan’s investigation desk can only review 40 claims per month. Rather than picking claims randomly or holding up hundreds of honest customers, our system automatically places the 40 highest-risk, highest-value claims directly onto their desks each month.

---

### 2. The Numbers

* **Board KPI (Accuracy):** **98.90%** (exceeds the Board's required 97.0% target).
* **The "Accuracy" Reality:** In warranty claims, 98.8% of all claims are honest. A system that approved 100% of claims would show 98.8% accuracy on paper, yet lose every single rupee of fraud! Our model delivers the 98.9% accuracy the Board expects, but specifically catches **97.2% of all fraudulent claims**.
* **Review Efficiency:** Farhan’s team reviews only **1.3 claims per business day**, completely respecting our staffing capacity.
